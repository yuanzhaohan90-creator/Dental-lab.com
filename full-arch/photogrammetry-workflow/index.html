Photogrammetry Full-Arch Digital Workflow

A reference workflow for scan flags, stitched implant data, exocad design, prototype review and reinforced final restoration planning.

This module is used to explain the digital checkpoints behind accurate full-arch implant records. It is not presented as a YZH-owned patient case.

Why This Matters

Full-arch accuracy depends on implant-position data.

In complex full-arch work, the laboratory needs reliable implant-position records before framework design and final production. Photogrammetry-style workflows are useful reference points for how scan flags, soft-tissue scans and pre-op records can be combined for planning.
