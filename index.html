Buyer Questions

Common concerns from laboratory owners and CAD/CAM managers.

Can you support lab-to-lab outsourcing instead of retail clinic work?

Yes. The workflow is written for dental laboratories that need overflow production, CAD support, implant capacity, or repeat monthly case handling.

Can we start with only one case?

Yes. A trial case is recommended. It lets your team judge communication, fit, finish, photo documentation, and delivery before larger volume.

Do you handle Exocad and implant cases?

Yes. Implant crowns, custom abutments, screw-retained restorations, bars, and full arch cases are core service categories.

What happens if information is missing?

We review the case first and ask for missing details before production, especially implant system, shade, material, margin, and screw access information.

Can you provide photos before shipment?

Yes. Final QC photos can be shared for new accounts, complex cases, and cases where the buyer wants review before packing.

Which buyers are the best fit?

Dental laboratories with 5-100 employees, growing digital workflows, technician shortages, or implant departments that need reliable outside support.
