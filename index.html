Why YZH Dental Lab

Real technical review before production, not just order taking.

Overseas labs need a partner that understands dental files, implant components, delivery pressure and remake risk. YZH keeps the first case simple, checks missing information early and communicates before production begins.

Experienced digital dental workflow for STL, PLY, Exocad and CAD/CAM production.

QC and final photo support for selected new or complex cases before shipment.

Digital Workflow

How to start a case with YZH.

A clear first-case workflow helps your team understand what to send, what we review, and when production starts.

01Send scans / files

STL, PLY, prescription, shade, photos, implant system and due date.

02Case review

We check missing details, risk points, material choice and approval needs.

03Design & confirmation

CAD design or technical confirmation is shared when the case requires approval.

04Production & QC

Milling, sintering, finishing, staining, polishing, fit check and inspection.

05Shipping

Final photos and dispatch details help your team manage customer expectations.
