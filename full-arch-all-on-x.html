Workflow

From records to final QC.

Records, technical review, design approval, final restoration and QC.

Records

Scans, bite, prescription, implant system and reference information are received.

2

Technical Review

3

Provisional / Design Approval

Technical Review

Complex full-arch cases are checked before manufacturing.

The goal is to identify missing or risky information early, not after a restoration has already been made.

Implant system and platform

Brand, connection and platform information are matched against the submitted files.

Scanbody / MUA information

Scanbody, multi-unit abutment and Ti-base information is reviewed when available.

Screw access and restorative space

Access channels, occlusal clearance and material thickness are checked.

Bite and provisional reference

Occlusion, midline, VDO and provisional references guide the design review.

What To Send

Send the records that let us review the case correctly.

If anything is missing, we will flag it before production instead of guessing.

  • STL / PLY scans and bite record
  • Prescription and requested material
  • Implant system, platform and tooth positions
  • Scanbody, MUA or Ti-base information
  • Provisional, wax-up, photos or design reference
  • Shade, due date and shipping requirements
