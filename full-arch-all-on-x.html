Six Full-Arch Modules

Built for cases that need more than routine production.

Each module describes a workflow your lab can outsource to YZH. For complex clinical situations, the clinician remains responsible for diagnosis, surgery and patient treatment decisions.

01

Dual-Arch All-on-X Rehabilitation

For upper and lower fixed full-arch cases where the lab needs coordinated esthetics, bite records, arch form and screw-access review.

  • Upper and lower arch design coordination
  • Midline, smile curve and vertical dimension review
  • Manufacturing notes before final approval

Visual: YZH laboratory capability image.

02

Zirconia on Titanium Bar

For demanding full-arch cases where a titanium support structure is planned under zirconia or hybrid restorative designs.

  • Bar shape, connector and restorative space review
  • Screw channel and cantilever risk communication
  • CAD screenshots for lab-to-lab approval

Visual: YZH titanium bar capability image.

03

Immediate-Load PMMA to Final Zirconia

For labs that receive provisional records first and need a controlled path toward a definitive zirconia restoration after tissue and bite confirmation.

  • Provisional contour replication guidance
  • Final zirconia design around approved records
  • Clear handoff between temporary and final phases

Visual: YZH full-arch restoration capability image.

FP1 Full-Arch Zirconia

For cases where the final restoration should preserve an FP1-style tooth-only appearance and requires strong control of emergence, contours and implant access.

Technical Module

Passive Fit / Verification support before the case becomes expensive.

This is not presented as a patient case. It is a technical checkpoint for laboratories that want seating, screw access, component information and framework design reviewed before committing to final manufacture.

01

Scanbody & Library Check

Implant brand, platform, scanbody and MUA or Ti-base details are reviewed when provided.

02

Verification Jig / Model Review

When records raise fit concerns, the case is flagged for verification instead of moving blindly to production.

03

Screw-Access Path

Access direction, restorative space and cantilever risks are communicated during design approval.

04

Final QC Handoff

Technicians check seating, interfaces, shade notes and shipping readiness before the case leaves the lab.

What To Send

Give our technicians the records needed for a useful review.

The fastest full-arch cases start with clear clinical and technical data. If something is missing, we will tell you before production starts.

  • Upper and lower scans or verified model scans
  • Bite record, photos and shade information
  • Implant system, platform, scanbody and MUA details
  • Provisional design, approved contours or duplicate scans when available
  • Preferred material path: PMMA, zirconia, titanium bar or hybrid
