Privacy Policy

This policy explains how YZH Dental Lab uses information submitted through this website.

Contact Information

When you submit your name, company, email, WhatsApp number or country, we use it to reply to your inquiry, discuss case details and provide dental laboratory support.

Uploaded Case Files

Uploaded files are used to evaluate the case, communicate technical requirements and support production discussion. Please use a lab case ID and avoid uploading unnecessary patient-identifying information.

Case ID

We recommend using a case ID in messages and files so communication can be handled without unnecessary personal patient information.

Retention and Sharing

Information is used for case communication and internal review. We do not sell submitted contact information. If a case cannot be reviewed through the form, we may ask you to resend files by email or WhatsApp.

File Types

The website accepts STL, PLY, ZIP, PDF, JPG and PNG uploads for case review. Large files may need to be shared by a secure link or direct communication.
